# G Madhan Prasad

Astrophysics student and researcher working at the intersection of observational astronomy, computational physics, and astronomical instrumentation. 

Currently an undergraduate researcher at **CHRIST (Deemed to be University)**, Bangalore, and a Project Student under the **National Initiative on Undergraduate Science (NIUS)** programme at the **Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR)**, Mumbai.

---

### Research Interests
- **Circumstellar & Protoplanetary Disks:** Episodic accretion phenomena, FUor/EXor-type outbursts, and young stellar object (YSO) evolution.
- **Astronomical Instrumentation & Calibration:** Near-Infrared (NIR) detector systematics, non-destructive Sample-Up-The-Ramp (SUTR) processing, and automated reduction architectures.
- **Orbital Mechanics & Exoplanetary Systems:** Resonance identification, orbital dynamics, and transit characterization.
